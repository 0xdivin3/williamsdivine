Who I Am

A builder, not
just a developer.

I'm Williams Divine Favour Chukwuebuka, a B.Sc. Computer Science graduate from Michael Okpara University of Agriculture. But my real education happened outside the classroom — through years of building, breaking, and rebuilding systems that actually matter.

My work sits at the intersection of AI/ML engineering, Python automation, and Web3 development. I've shipped trading bots, autonomous AI agents, published Python packages, built mobile apps, and deployed real products used by real people — all while self-teaching every step of the way.

I have a background in graphic design and trading (Smart Money Concept methodology), which gives me a unique edge: I think in systems, design with intention, and build with markets in mind. I don't build todo apps — I build tools.

I also teach. I've facilitated the ICT track of a skill acquisition program in Delta State, taking students through web design, AI, graphic design and digital marketing with practical, hands-on sessions.
